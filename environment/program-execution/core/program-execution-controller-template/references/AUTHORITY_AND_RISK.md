# Authority and Risk

Effective action permission is the intersection of policy, exact approval, Blueprint ceiling, Source Contract, and Rendered Contract. Any disagreement narrows permission or blocks execution.

- T0: inspect only.
- T1: reversible documentation, tests, or metadata.
- T2: reversible implementation on one target.
- T3: compatibility-sensitive or partially reversible work; local proof may proceed, promotion pauses.
- T4: evidence and planning only until exact destructive approval and recovery evidence exist.
