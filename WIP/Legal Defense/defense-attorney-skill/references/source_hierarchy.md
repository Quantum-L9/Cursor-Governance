# Source Hierarchy

Use this hierarchy for every North Carolina legal proposition.

## Tier 0 — controlling / primary research targets
1. **North Carolina Supreme Court and Court of Appeals official opinions and dockets** — `appellate.nccourts.org`
2. **North Carolina General Assembly enacted statutes** — `ncleg.gov`
3. **North Carolina Judicial Branch** rules, forms, local orders, calendars, eCourts/File & Serve materials — `nccourts.gov`
4. **Official court record / eCourts Portal / filed instruments** for case-specific posture and charging language.
5. **United States Supreme Court official materials** for controlling federal constitutional precedent when applicable.

Prefer the actual opinion, statute, order, rule, filed instrument, or docket over a summary.

## Tier 1 — expert secondary sources
Use for explanation, issue spotting, procedural context, and finding primary authority:
- UNC School of Government criminal law blog
- UNC Defender Manual
- NC PRO / SOG manuals
- North Carolina Indigent Defense Services materials

Do not let a secondary source override conflicting primary authority.

## Tier 2 — research aids
- FindLaw
- Justia
- other commercial/free case-law mirrors

Use for navigation and convenience. Verify material propositions against Tier 0 before treating them as controlling or quoting exact language.

## User-created / AI-created materials
The case handoff, project instructions, prior AI outputs, draft filings, and bundled precedent cards are **working materials**, not legal authority. Preserve their useful structure, but verify material law and live procedure against higher-tier sources.

## Conflict rule
When sources conflict:
1. prefer higher-tier authority;
2. prefer later effective controlling authority within the same tier;
3. prefer the actual filed or docketed case record for case-state facts;
4. mark unresolved conflicts `CONTRADICTION` or `STATUS_UNKNOWN`.
