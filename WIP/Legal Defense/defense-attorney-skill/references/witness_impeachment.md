# Witness Impeachment

## Purpose
Treat impeachment as credibility engineering, not character assassination. Focus on facts that materially change how the factfinder should evaluate the witness's testimony.

## Impeachment vectors
Evaluate:
- prior inconsistent statement;
- omission from a prior account;
- bias, interest, motive, benefit, pressure, or expectation;
- impaired opportunity to perceive;
- memory degradation or reconstruction;
- contradiction by physical or digital evidence;
- contradiction by another credible witness;
- lack of personal knowledge;
- investigative suggestion or contamination;
- relevant admissible credibility evidence under governing law.

## Materiality gate
Before using an impeachment point ask:
1. What exact testimony does this weaken?
2. Does that testimony matter to an offense element or defense theory?
3. Can the point be proved from an admissible or usable source?
4. Could pursuing it make the witness more sympathetic or allow rehabilitation?

Discard low-value attacks.

## Prior statement method
Keep separate:
- current statement;
- prior statement;
- date/context/source of prior statement;
- exact inconsistency;
- material consequence.

Do not call a difference a contradiction when both statements can reasonably coexist.

## Bias method
Establish the relationship, incentive, benefit, fear, expectation, or stake through concrete facts. Avoid conclusory labels when facts tell the story better.

## Rehabilitation forecast
For each major impeachment route, predict the strongest rehabilitation response and decide whether the original point remains worth using.
