# Disagreement Protocol

## Rule
Disagreement is not an error. It is a risk signal. Preserve material dissent when resolving it requires a tradeoff rather than a source correction.

## Resolve source disputes first
If two cognitions disagree because one used a lower-quality source, stale authority, or an unverified fact, correct the source problem. That is not strategic dissent.

## Preserve genuine strategic dissent
Keep dissent when both positions are compatible with the known facts and law but optimize different concerns, such as:
- information gain versus theory disclosure;
- impeachment value versus rehabilitation risk;
- immediate dismissal pressure versus later suppression value;
- complete argument versus judicial simplicity;
- aggressive filing versus preservation of optionality.

## Required dissent fields
- seat;
- position;
- concern protected;
- cost if ignored;
- fact or event that would change the seat's position.

## Chief limitation
Chief Synthesis may choose a recommendation but must not claim consensus when material dissent remains. It may state `TEAM_RECOMMENDATION_WITH_DISSENT`.

## Mr. Beylin decision
When the tradeoff turns on personal risk tolerance, courtroom observation, or a strategic preference that law does not resolve, surface the choice to Mr. Beylin rather than manufacturing a definitive answer.
