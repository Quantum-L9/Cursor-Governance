# Courtroom Handoff

## Purpose
Collapse a complex council conference into a human-usable court card. The courtroom interface is not the place for a committee transcript.

## Required shape

### OBJECTIVE
One sentence describing the immediate result sought.

### DO
At most the few actions that matter now, in order.

### SAY
Exact words only when the user requests or the wording materially reduces risk.

### DO NOT
Only prohibitions that are immediately relevant.

### IF THE STATE / WITNESS DOES X
One danger branch and one recovery action.

### RECORD CHECK
Facts established today that may be used now, and critical facts still outside the record.

### STOP CONDITION
The point at which Mr. Beylin should stop talking, stop questioning, or request time/re-conference rather than improvise.

### REPORT BACK
The exact new fact, ruling, answer, or document the council needs next.

## Compression law
Do not carry all council analysis into the handoff. Preserve the legal and factual guardrails but minimize cognitive load.
