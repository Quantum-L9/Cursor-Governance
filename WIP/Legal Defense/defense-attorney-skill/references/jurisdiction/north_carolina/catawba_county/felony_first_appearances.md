# Catawba Felony First Appearances and Related Superior Court Dates

## Authority
Administrative Order Addressing First Appearances in Felony Cases and the Setting of Court Dates in Other Matters in Superior Court.

- Filed: 2025-12-05
- Effective: 2026-01-01
- Court: Catawba County Superior Court, Judicial District 19
- Judge: Senior Resident Superior Court Judge Nathaniel J. Poovey
- Supersedes: amended administrative order entered 2016-04-25 concerning felony first appearances
- Pack verified through: 2026-08-12

## New indictments
### Arrest order path
For categories described in paragraph 2(a), including specified situations involving no prior same-felony warrant, no waiver of probable cause, or a district-court finding of no probable cause, the clerk is directed to issue an order for arrest on the new indictment. Upon arrest, the magistrate sets the first appearance in the next available Superior Criminal Court session under the order's scheduling rules.

### No new arrest order path
Where the defendant had been charged with the same felony by warrant and either waived probable cause or District Court found probable cause, paragraph 2(b) directs the clerk not to issue an arrest order on the indictment and to set the matter for the next available Criminal Session of Superior Court pursuant to the calendar provided by the District Attorney.

## Arrest timing rule under paragraph 3
For arrests under paragraph 2(a):
- arrested before 12:00 p.m. on the last business day of the week: first appearance is set for the following week of Superior Court if Superior Court is in session;
- arrested after 12:00 p.m. on the last business day: first appearance is set for the next available Superior Court calendar after the following week.

At the Superior Court first appearance, the judge also addresses matters identified in N.C.G.S. §§ 15A-602 through 15A-605 and gives the next court date, set for the next available Criminal Administrative Session under the District Attorney-provided calendar.

## Probation violations
The order continues local procedures for statutory preliminary hearings on new Superior Court probation violations and sets scheduling rules for probation-violation failures to appear and probation-officer citations.

## Misdemeanor appeals
- appeal noticed in open District Court: the Assistant District Attorney is to advise the court and defendant of the Superior Court trial date and ensure calendaring;
- timely appeal noticed outside open court: the clerk sets the Superior Court trial calendar date **not earlier than 30 days after notice of appeal is filed** and transmits notice to the District Attorney's office.

## Superior Court failure to appear
Upon arrest for failure to appear for a Superior Court date, the magistrate sets the matter for the next available Superior Court criminal session under the District Attorney-provided calendar.

## Arraignment
The order directs Superior Court arraignments required by N.C.G.S. § 15A-943 to occur **on or before the third administrative court date** for each Superior Court criminal case unless continued by joint consent of the State and defendant.
