# Catawba County Criminal Procedure

## Governing concept
Catawba procedure is North Carolina criminal procedure plus valid local rules/orders applicable to Superior Court District 19 and District Court District 36.

Do not create a separate body of substantive "Catawba criminal law." State substantive law and controlling appellate precedent remain North Carolina law unless a federal issue controls.

## Case-stage checklist
Before advising on local procedure, identify:
- Catawba venue established? yes/no/UNKNOWN
- charge type: felony/misdemeanor/infraction/UNKNOWN
- indictment returned? yes/no/UNKNOWN
- probable-cause hearing held/waived? status
- represented by counsel? status
- custody/arrest status
- current court division
- next scheduled session/date if known
- whether matter involves probation violation, misdemeanor appeal, FTA, or arraignment

## Felony path
Use Chapter 15A as the statewide baseline. When the 2025 District 19 administrative order applies to a new indictment or one of its enumerated matters, apply its local scheduling mechanics as summarized in `felony_first_appearances.md`.

## Misdemeanors
Misdemeanor trials generally proceed in District Court. A defendant convicted in District Court may appeal to Superior Court for trial de novo subject to current law and deadlines. The District 19 order contains local scheduling directions for misdemeanor appeals in Superior Court.

## Calendars
Treat a calendar date as a case-specific fact. Do not infer it from this pack. Retrieve the current Catawba criminal calendar or court record when available.
