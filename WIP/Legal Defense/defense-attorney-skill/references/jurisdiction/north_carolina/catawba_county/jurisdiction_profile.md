## Trial-court routing
### District Court
Primarily handles misdemeanor and infraction trials and preliminary felony proceedings under North Carolina law.

### Superior Court
Handles felony trials and misdemeanor/infraction appeals from District Court, plus other matters assigned by law.

Do not infer the operative court solely from the offense label. Identify indictment status, charging instrument, procedural stage, appeal status, and any transfer/plea mechanism.
