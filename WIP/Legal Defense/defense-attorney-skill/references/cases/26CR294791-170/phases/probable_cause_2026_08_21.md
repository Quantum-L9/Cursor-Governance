## Governing procedural anchor
Bundled verification snapshot of N.C.G.S. § 15A-611 confirms: witnesses testify under oath and are subject to cross; the State generally proceeds with nonhearsay or hearsay-exception evidence subject to enumerated exceptions; expert reports have a statutory exception; the district judge is not required to exclude evidence because it was acquired unlawfully.

Bundled verification snapshot of N.C.G.S. § 15A-612 confirms the judge may bind over, reduce to a qualifying lesser district-court offense, or must dismiss if no probable cause exists under subdivisions (1) or (2); a finding does not bar later prosecution for the same offense.

## Record objective
Treat cross as both a merits tool and a record-building event. Use `record_boundary.yaml` so closing argument rests on testimony/evidence actually in the hearing record, not the private affidavit or anticipated proof.

## Search/odor analysis
If the State relies on odor or vehicle-search facts, compare the testimony against Dobson and Rowdy factor-by-factor. Freeze facts at the relevant police-action moment. Do not use later-discovered contraband to justify an earlier decision unless the governing law actually permits that temporal use for the specific proposition.

## Transition triggers

- an indictment or other Superior Court jurisdictional event makes the PC hearing unavailable;
- the PC hearing concludes;
- the user explicitly changes the active objective.
