# Phase — Post PC Disposition / District Court Branch

## If dismissed under N.C.G.S. § 15A-612(a)(3)
Treat the dismissal as non-final with respect to the State's ability to institute a subsequent prosecution because § 15A-612(b) expressly preserves that ability. Do not describe the matter as permanently over solely because of the PC dismissal.

## If reduced to a lesser district-court offense
Verify the exact new pleading/offense and § 15A-613 posture before advising on trial, plea, or calendaring.

## If the State voluntarily dismisses
Verify the actual disposition, any refiling risk, and the current expunction statute before discussing expunction eligibility or finality.

## Always
Use the live docket and filed order/disposition rather than the user's recollection alone.
