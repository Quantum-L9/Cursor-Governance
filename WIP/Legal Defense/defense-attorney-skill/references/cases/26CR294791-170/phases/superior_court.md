# Phase — Superior Court

## Activation
Activate only after current official case posture confirms Superior Court jurisdiction, such as a true bill of indictment or another valid jurisdictional route.

## Reset before analysis
- verify the caption and case number format
- obtain/verify the charging instrument
- rebuild the charge-element map from current law
- recalculate discovery and motion deadlines
- distinguish testimony captured at any prior probable-cause hearing from later discovery
- revalidate Dobson/Rowdy/Schiene status
- revisit suppression under N.C.G.S. § 15A-977 and applicable constitutional doctrine

## Do not inherit blindly
Probable-cause-phase tactical restrictions do not automatically govern Superior Court. Re-evaluate subpoenas, discovery, suppression, inventory/chain issues, expert needs, and witness strategy from the new posture.
