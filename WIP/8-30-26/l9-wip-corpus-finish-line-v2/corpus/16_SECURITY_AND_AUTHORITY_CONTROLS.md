# Security and Authority Controls

- Producers cannot write Graphiti directly.
- Publication payload identity must bind effect identity and candidate semantics.
- Memory principal comes from runtime/operator authority, never corpus payload.
- Source-secret candidate files are observed only to the allowed metadata level and are not semantically interpreted.
- Symlinks and archive expansion remain bounded and fail closed.
- Dropbox source trees are read-only to corpus intelligence stages.
- Candidate analysis never grants canonical status.
- Program Execution receives only the authority explicitly contained in its campaign/handoff contract.
- Deletion/privacy erasure is distinct from corpus retraction/temporal invalidation.
