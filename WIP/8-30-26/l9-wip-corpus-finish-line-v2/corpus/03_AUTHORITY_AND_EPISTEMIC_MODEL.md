# Authority and Epistemic Model

Every relationship must preserve its class:

- ASSERTED: source explicitly declared it.
- DETERMINISTIC: mechanically decided, e.g. exact content-hash duplicate.
- CANONICAL_DERIVED: topology derived it under versioned rules and validation.
- CANDIDATE: semantic/topic/project/consolidation analysis proposed it.
- UNKNOWN: insufficient evidence or unresolved target.

Candidate relations never silently become canonical edges. Retrieval may use candidates for discovery but must label them. Planning may reason over candidates but cannot present them as hard prerequisites without promotion evidence.

Graphiti may help find and rank candidates. Canonical Memory hydration is the authority for evidence, confidence method, temporal state, lifecycle, provenance, and conflict status.
