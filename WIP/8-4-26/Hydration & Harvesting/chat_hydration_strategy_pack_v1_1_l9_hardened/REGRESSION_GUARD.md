## Validation Guard

Every future artifact added to the pack must be:

1. Registered in `00_manifest/MANIFEST.yaml`.
2. Included in `FINAL_REPO_TREE.md`.
3. Covered by validation or marked `UNKNOWN`.
4. Aligned to mobile hydration scope.
