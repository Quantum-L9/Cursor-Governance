# First-Order Leverage

Ask: What is the smallest change that delivers the most functional value and improves future moves?

Evaluate:

- functional impact versus cosmetic change;
- future work unlocked;
- reuse across tasks or systems;
- recurring maintenance created;
- reversibility and optionality;
- evidence needed before commitment.

Prefer a reusable decision primitive over another checklist. Prefer one discriminating probe over three ceremonial searches. Prefer a specific domain contract over duplicated general doctrine.

Label leverage claims `Unknown` when they are not evidenced.
